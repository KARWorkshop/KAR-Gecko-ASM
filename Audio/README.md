Various codes used for audio

- Disable Music || Stops the music from playing

- Split Audio Abadoned || a attempt at porting the split audio code but was abandoned

- Mute Fire At Least || mutes the fire emitting sound at least (maybe other stuff)

- Random Course Music AR CT || plays random course music in Air Ride and City Trial

- Play Legendary Piece FX When Red Box || when a legendary piece is found, play the FX

- CT Music No Change In Last 30s || music won't change in CT at the last 30 seconds

- Disable event alarm || disables the event alarm
