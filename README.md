# KAR-Gecko-ASM
Repo for all the ASM and Gecko Codes used with KARphin.

Some of theses codes have been developed over time and thus have been tweaked heavilly and have older variants. This repo is mainly for KARphin and it's extra codes. For KARphin Legacy codes they are archived but won't be updated. KARphin's have been cleaned up and changed from that one. Mainly restoring memory cards and booting right into the debug menu. **Assume North American or modded variant unless otherwise stated.**

- LegacyGeckoCodeFiles || All the codes shipped with the legacy client, not organized.

- LegacyASM || old ASM of the codes recover, kept for historical sake.

- Modifier Guide || this contains codes that need specific modifers and the old guide from the KAR.de site. Theses codes aren't really used but should be archived and or make a prog to generate them so they can be used in the future.

- CT_NetplayCore || All the core codes used in netplay, always enabled.

- CT_Fullscreen || All the screen codes and specific screen codes. Auto-loaded by KARphin

- BootMenu || Codes for booting into various menus at launch

- CT_StarSpawns || Affects star Spawns

- CT_Stadium || Affects the stadiums.

- Debug || various debug codes

- Audio || various codes that affect audio

- CT_StarCamping || Codes that stop star camping

- CT_CustomInput || codes for custom input for actions, some are built in now a days

- CT_StarModifiers || affects Stars that spawn

- AI || codes for various AIs

- CT_Eevnts || various codes for CT events

- Mic || codes that have no good place or needs to be organized

- Outdated Codes || super old codes that are never used or have newer versions, we keep theses for the sake of history.

- Rider || codes that affect Riders

- CT_Items || codes that affect Items

- CT_PowerUps || codes that affect power ups

- CT_Map || codes that affect maps
