Star Spawns, some are banned in comp play. Only one star code can exist at a time.

- No Swerve Star || Stops the swerve star from spawning

- BS Custom Star Spawn || Spawns the Lance and Solar Star, only useable in Backside B2/B3 or if you have the stars enabled as mods.

- BS Custom Star Spawn And No Swerve Star || Spawns the Lance and Solar Star and stops the swerve star from spawning in one code.
